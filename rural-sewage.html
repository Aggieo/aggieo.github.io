Project Context
Background & Policy Mandate

The host city has pursued systematic wastewater improvement since 2014 under Zhejiang's "Five-Water Co-governance" programme. This project responds to the provincial Strong Foundation, Double Standard Action Plan (2021–2025), which mandates ≥95% administrative village coverage and ≥95% effluent compliance by 2025.

The four villages comprise 10 natural hamlets across roughly 277 km² of administrative area. Before this project, none of the hamlets had a sewer network — domestic wastewater discharged directly to natural watercourses, which is exactly the condition the provincial programme targets.

Design & Field Work Process
From Technical Proposal to Construction Supervision
01
Technical Proposal

Prepared the technical proposal that secured the design contract.

02
Field Investigation

Six weeks on site: topographic and elevation survey (51–124 m), drainage mapping, and door-to-door visits to 1,000+ households to assess septic tanks, kitchen facilities and wastewater generation.

03
Preliminary & Detailed Design

Authored the preliminary design report and produced detailed (construction) drawings: network layout, longitudinal profiles, six pump stations (300 t/d combined), LID integration, energy analysis.

04
Quantities & Cost Estimate

Quantity take-off from the detailed drawings and cost estimate.

05
Construction Supervision

Provided on-site design support during construction — resolving drawing queries, clarifying specifications, and ensuring pipe installation aligned with design intent.
