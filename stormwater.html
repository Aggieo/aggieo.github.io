01 · Site Overview

Project Background

  • Southwest London, Ontario — formerly agricultural land
  • Catchment area: 22.82 ha, high water table
  • Ground elevation: 254–260 m ASL (mostly flat)
  • Proposed end-of-pipe facility: wet pond
  • Includes stormwater routing, catch basins & wetpond design
  • Modelled with Visual OTTHYMO using City of London IDF parameters

02 · Design Objectives

Scope & Goals

  • Flood control — store runoff during 2–250 yr storms; release within 24–48 hrs
  • Runoff detention — controlled release, preventing thermal loading
  • Water quality — remove sediments, nutrients & heavy metals in forebay
  • Compliance — City of London IDF, OPSD/OPSS standards, Ontario MOE
  • Amenity — trails, birdwatching, landscape integration

06 · Key Findings & Conclusion
Hydrological Impact

Post-development peak flows increased up to 10× while time-to-peak decreased sharply, highlighting the critical need for stormwater detention.

Pond Performance

The designed wet pond successfully attenuates peak discharge across all storm events (25 mm – 250 yr), restoring post-outlet flows to near pre-development levels.

Regulatory Compliance

Design adheres to City of London SWM guidelines, OPSD/OPSS standards, and Ontario MOE Stormwater Management Planning & Design Manual.
