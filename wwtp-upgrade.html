Key Scope of Works
  • New denitrification deep-bed filter (tertiary treatment) for enhanced nitrogen removal
  • Biochemical pool converted from AO to AAO to improve TN & TP removal efficiency
  • Coarse screen well and lift pump station equipment upgraded; new reject screen installed
  • Chemical dosing room and dewatering machine room renovated — plate-frame press replaced, automatic sludge conveyor added
  • SCADA control system upgraded; transformer capacity increased to 400 kVA with backup power
  • New biological odour control system covering screen rooms and sludge handling area
  • Pump power re-evaluated and retrofitted, optimising energy efficiency and cutting electricity use by 10%

Design Challenges & Engineering Response
  • Tightened provincial NH₃-N and TP limits — met by converting the biological train from AO to AAO and adding a tertiary deep-bed denitrification filter
  • Influent pumps oversized for the actual duty point — ratings re-evaluated against the system curve and units retrofitted, cutting plant electricity use by 10%
  • Oxygen transfer below design — blowers and diffuser membranes replaced to restore aeration capacity
  • Sludge dewatering throughput limiting the solids line — plate-frame press replaced and an automatic sludge conveyor added
  • Hydraulic short-circuiting in the anaerobic zone — baffle arrangement and flow distribution revised
