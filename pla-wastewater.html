The composite stream combines process condensates, scrubber blowdown, equipment and floor washings, cooling blowdown and recycled RO concentrate. The governing design challenge is a low B/C ratio: PLA oligomers and lactide residues hydrolyse slowly, so a conventional activated-sludge train alone cannot reach the discharge limit — hence the hydrolysis-acidification stage ahead of the biological units and RO polishing at the tail.
